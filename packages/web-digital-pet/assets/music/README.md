# Web soundtrack

The web app plays **Butter-Fly** while you explore and **Brave Heart** during
battles, with both songs arranged in an 8-bit style by **BgmManJX**.

Music starts with your first interaction. Turn it on or off in **Options → Music**;
your choice is remembered. It also works offline and pauses when you leave the app.
