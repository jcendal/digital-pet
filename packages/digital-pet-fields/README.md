# Digital Pet Fields

Explore the Digital World through ten destinations, each with its own pixel
landscape, inhabitants, and regional bosses.

| Field | Destination |
| --- | --- |
| Nature Spirits | Gear Savannah |
| Deep Savers | Coela Beach and Dragon Eye Lake |
| Nightmare Soldiers | Wasteland |
| Wind Guardians | Digital Forest |
| Metal Empire | Digital City |
| Virus Busters | Village of Beginnings |
| Dragon's Roar | Ancient Dino Region |
| Jungle Troopers | Tropical Jungle |
| Dark Area | Vamdemon Castle |
| Unknown | Upside-Down Pyramid |

Your journey begins at **Dragon Eye Lake**. In the web app,
select the place name above your companion or open **Options → Your World** to
browse destinations and travel.

The habitat guide shows the Digimon that live in each region and marks those
you have raised. A species can appear in several regions. Visiting a place does
not unlock its inhabitants in the Digidex.

Adventures can bring encounters with regional bosses inspired by *Digimon
Adventure*, *Digimon Frontier*, and their films. Defeat one to earn a star on its
region's map card.

See the [web app guide](../web-digital-pet/README.md) for travel and adventure
controls. Contributors can find data sources and asset guidance in the
[world catalog guide](docs/catalog.md).
