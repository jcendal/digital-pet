# Digital Pet Fields

Explore the Digital World through twenty destinations in ten habitats, each with
its own pixel landscape. Every habitat has its own inhabitants and regional bosses.

| Field | Destinations |
| --- | --- |
| Nature Spirits | Gear Savannah and Geko Swamp |
| Deep Savers | Coela Beach and Dragon Eye Lake |
| Nightmare Soldiers | Wasteland and Overdell Cemetery |
| Wind Guardians | Digital Forest and Mount Panorama |
| Metal Empire | Digital City and Factorial Town |
| Virus Busters | Village of Beginnings and Ice Sanctuary |
| Dragon's Roar | Ancient Dino Region and Great Canyon |
| Jungle Troopers | Tropical Jungle and Mangrove Region |
| Dark Area | Vamdemon Castle and Dark Terminal |
| Unknown | Upside-Down Pyramid and Toy Mansion |

Your journey begins at **Dragon Eye Lake**. In the web app, open **Map** or select
the place name above your companion to travel. **Options → Your World** lets you
browse each habitat and its two destinations.

The habitat guide shows the Digimon that live in each region and marks those
you have raised. A species can appear in several regions. Visiting a place does
not unlock its inhabitants in the Digidex.

Adventures can bring encounters with regional bosses inspired by *Digimon
Adventure*, *Digimon Frontier*, and their films. Defeat one to earn a star on its
region's map card.

See the [web app guide](../web-digital-pet/README.md) for travel and adventure
controls. Contributors can find data sources and asset guidance in the
[world catalog guide](docs/catalog.md).
